### Hatch for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Hatch is a command in AutoCAD that is used to fill an area of selected objects with a hatch pattern or fill.
- Hatch patterns or fills represent materials to be used and incorporated within a design, such as concrete, wood, brick, etc.
- Hatch can also be used to designate areas or room types, such as office, kitchen, bathroom, etc.
- Hatch command can be accessed from the Draw menu of the Home tab in the ribbon.
- To use the hatch command, follow these steps:
  - Select the Hatch command from the Draw menu or type HATCH in the command line.
  - In the Hatch Creation tab, choose a hatch pattern or fill from the Pattern panel. You can also adjust the scale, angle, and origin of the pattern or fill.
  - In the Boundaries panel, choose a boundary selection method, such as Pick Points, Select Objects, or Create Boundary Set. You can also enable or disable the Island Detection option, which determines how the hatch fills the enclosed areas.
  - In the drawing area, select the objects or points that define the boundary of the hatch area. You can also use object snaps or other tools to specify precise locations.
  - Press Enter to confirm the hatch area and exit the command.
- You can modify the hatch properties, such as layer, color, transparency, etc., from the Properties panel in the Hatch Creation tab.
- You can also edit the hatch boundary or associate the hatch to a new boundary from the Modify panel in the Hatch Creation tab.
- To follow the GSA Computer-Aided Design Standards, you must use the United States National CAD Standard® (NCS) for hatch patterns or fills. The NCS provides a uniform set of symbols, layers, linetypes, and hatch patterns for different disciplines and purposes.
