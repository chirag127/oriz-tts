### DDoS Attacks

- A DDoS attack is a distributed denial-of-service attack that targets websites and servers by disrupting network services .
- A DDoS attack attempts to exhaust an application's resources by flooding it with HTTP requests and traffic from multiple compromised computer systems, also known as bots or botnets .
- A DDoS attack can result in degraded network performance, service delay, or service outage of critical infrastructure.
- A DDoS attack can have various motives, such as cybercrime, hacktivism, cyberwarfare, or personal revenge.
- A DDoS attack can use different techniques, such as volumetric, protocol, or application layer attacks, to overwhelm the target or its surrounding infrastructure .
- A DDoS attack can be prevented or mitigated by using various methods, such as firewalls, load balancing, traffic filtering, or DDoS protection services  .
